Swapped houses: the king and the soldier trade places

Four parts: the sky, the swap, who adds what, the result

PART 1 · THE SKY
On Monday 16 November at 9:13 AM Toronto time the Sun (the king) crosses from Libra into Scorpio. That crossing is a Sankranti, Sanskrit for "crossing over". There are twelve a year, and Makar Sankranti in January is the famous one. He leaves Libra, his weakest sign (Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, chapter 3), and stays in Scorpio until 15 December.
Scorpio belongs to Mars (the soldier), who already lives in Leo, the king's own sign. Each one is a guest in the other's house.

PART 2 · THE SWAP
The classics call this parivartana, an exchange of signs (Phaladeepika, chapter 6). Nadi reads it in three steps (Nadi Jyotisha, volume 1, chapter 5)
Step 1, two grahas (Sanskrit for "the ones who grab") sit in each other's signs
Step 2, send each one home in your mind: the king to Leo, the soldier to Scorpio. At home both count as strong at first
Step 3, see who each one meets there

PART 3 · WHO ADDS WHAT
In Leo the king meets the teacher (Jupiter), who adds advice all month, and the monk (Ketu), who is on his way out. On 2 December the messenger (Mercury) walks into Scorpio and adds talk.

PART 4 · THE RESULT
Authority gets its nerve back, and the call stuck since October gets made. Drive finds one clear target. Power takes advice, so ask a mentor before the big move. Write it down and check it on 15 December.
