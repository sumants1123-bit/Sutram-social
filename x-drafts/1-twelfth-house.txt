Most people fear the 12th house: loss, bills, hospitals
Here is a more useful way to read it. The 12th house shows what you have to spend, give up or switch off from, so you can keep going.

For one person it's sleep: they only work well on eight hours, and every short night costs them the next day
For another it's distance: they built a life in a new city or country, and the price is missing family dinners and festivals back home
For another it's quiet time: a weekend offline, a prayer or meditation hour, giving money to a cause

The old texts give the 12th house two jobs: your expenses and your sleep. That pairing makes sense in city life. When you don't rest, you pay for it anyway: the late-night takeout, the cancelled plans, the clinic visit, the bad decision made tired.

Sutra:
"What you won't spend on rest, you will spend on recovery."

A strong 12th house often makes you good at letting go: switching off, travelling, living abroad, healing work, helping people quietly.

Practical remedy:
Open your bank app and look at the last three months. Circle every charge that came from being tired or burnt out, and add it up. That number is what skipping rest costs you. Now put two hours of real rest in your calendar this week, before anything else gets booked.
