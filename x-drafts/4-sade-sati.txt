Sade Sati is running right now for three Moon signs: Aquarius, Pisces and Aries
Saturn spends about two and a half years in each sign. Sade Sati is the stretch when it passes through three signs in a row: the one before your Moon sign, your Moon sign, and the one after. Three signs, about seven and a half years.

People call it bad luck. Saturn is the auditor among the grahas, and Sade Sati works like an audit at work. The auditor checks what you built and how you built it. Things built on shortcuts get tested. Things built on steady effort usually hold.

For one person it's a job they stayed in out of fear, which finally stops working
For another it's health habits running on credit: years of late nights and skipped meals that start sending the bill
For another it's a relationship on autopilot, which now needs real attention

Sutra:
"Saturn takes away what you were only holding out of habit."

What Saturn rewards: routine, patience, honest work, and care for elders and for the people who work for you

Where you are now:
Aquarius Moon: the last stretch, ending early 2028
Pisces Moon: the middle and heaviest part, until June 2027
Aries Moon: just started, running to 2032

Practical remedy:
Write down three things you keep doing only out of habit. Drop one this month. Then give every Saturday one hour of real work on the thing you keep putting off. Saturday is Saturn's day, and Saturn respects people who show up.
