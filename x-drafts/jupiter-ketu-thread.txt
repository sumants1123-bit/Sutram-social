1/11
Jupiter meets Ketu.

The teacher walks into the monk's room, and the soldier is on the way.

From 13 to 16 November, Jupiter, Ketu and Mars are within one degree of each other. Here's the story, read the Nadi way, and what it means for each Ascendant.

2/11
In Bhrigu Nandi Nadi, the grahas are characters in a story. Graha is Sanskrit for "the one who grabs": each one grabs a part of your life.

Jupiter is the teacher: what you were taught, your faith, your savings
Ketu is the monk: what you already know, and what you're done with
Mars is the soldier: the push to act, and the temper

They meet in Magha, Ketu's own star, whose symbol is a throne and whose deity is the ancestors.

3/11
The rest of the cast:

Leo belongs to the king (Sun), who is away in Libra and weak.
The messenger (Mercury) and the treasurer (Venus) walk backward and swap houses.
The auditor (Saturn) walks back to sit with the magician (Rahu), and both face the room.

In Nadi, a graha walking backward also acts from the sign behind it.

4/11
Act 1, the doorway (31 Oct to 11 Nov)

The teacher steps into Leo on 31 Oct through Gandanta, the knot where a water sign meets a fire sign. The room is Magha, the monk's own star, and Ketu is already inside (exact 11 to 12 Nov). The soldier follows behind, at his weakest on Diwali.

Hemmed between them, what you only borrowed from books, bosses and gurus gets tested against what you actually lived.

5/11
Act 2, the knot (12 to 18 Nov)

The soldier crosses the same doorway on 12 Nov. From 13 to 16 Nov, teacher, monk and soldier are within 1°. On 13 Nov the messenger and the treasurer stop walking backward.

Stuck decisions move fast. People quit, break up or speak up for what they believe. Leaders and institutions face a test. Drive, cook and speak with care.

6/11
Act 3, the release (5 to 12 Dec)

The monk steps back into Cancer on 5 Dec, the auditor turns forward on 10 Dec, and the teacher stops to look back on 12 Dec. What was borrowed falls away; what's yours stays.

It isn't over. Jupiter meets Ketu twice more, about 1 Mar and 19 Apr 2027. Between now and June, Jupiter, Mars and Ketu cross this doorway seven times.

7/11

Gemini: Your 3rd: courage, posts, pitches, siblings. Jupiter rules your 7th and 10th, so a partner or boss is in it. Your messenger, Mercury, turns forward 13 Nov: send after that.

11/11
How to use it:
Until 13 Nov, finish things; don't sign or launch.
13 to 16 Nov, keep big decisions out of it; drive and speak with care.
After 5 Dec, keep what you've lived and let the rest go.

You cannot carry the old skin and sit straight at the same time.
