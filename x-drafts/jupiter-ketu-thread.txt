1/10
Jupiter meets Ketu at the doorway between Cancer and Leo, and Mars arrives right behind.

From 13 to 16 November, all three are within one degree of each other.

Here's the story, read the Nadi way, and what it means for each Ascendant.

2/10
In Bhrigu Nandi Nadi, planets are characters in a story.

Jupiter is the teacher: what you were taught, your faith, your savings
Ketu is the monk: what you already know, and what you're done with
Mars is the soldier: the push to act, and the temper

They meet in Magha, Ketu's own star, named for the throne of the ancestors.

3/10
Act 1, the doorway (31 Oct to 11 Nov)

Jupiter steps into Leo on 31 Oct through Gandanta, the knot where a water sign meets a fire sign. Ketu waits just ahead (exact 11 to 12 Nov). Mars follows behind, at its weakest on Diwali.

Hemmed between them, what you only borrowed from books, bosses and gurus gets tested against what you actually lived.

4/10
Act 2, the knot (12 to 18 Nov)

Mars crosses the same doorway on 12 Nov. From 13 to 16 Nov, Mars, Jupiter and Ketu are within 1°, and Mercury and Venus both turn forward on 13 Nov.

Stuck decisions move fast. People quit, break up or speak up for what they believe. Leaders and institutions face a test. Drive, cook and speak with care.

5/10
Act 3, the release (5 to 12 Dec)

Ketu slips back into Cancer on 5 Dec, Saturn turns forward on 10 Dec, and Jupiter pauses on 12 Dec. What was borrowed falls away; what's yours stays.

It isn't over. Jupiter meets Ketu twice more, about 1 Mar and 19 Apr 2027. Between now and June, Jupiter, Mars and Ketu cross this doorway seven times.

6/10
By Ascendant (1/4)

Aries: It lands in your 5th: kids, love, studies, a risky bet. Your own planet Mars joins on 12 Nov, so don't bet big that week.

Taurus: Your 4th: home, mother, a lease or a move. Home talks get heated mid-month (Mars rules your 7th). Fix the house first.

Gemini: Your 3rd: courage, posts, pitches, siblings. Jupiter rules your 7th and 10th, so a partner or boss is in it. Your Mercury turns forward 13 Nov: send after that.

7/10
By Ascendant (2/4)

Cancer: Your 2nd: savings, family, how you speak. Mars sits on you, at its weakest, until 12 Nov, so you're tired and touchy. Budget, don't brag.

Leo: It happens on you. An old version of you drops away: your look, routine or direction. Mars joins on 12 Nov: big energy, short fuse. Mind your driving.

Virgo: Your 12th: sleep, spending, travel abroad, letting go. A home or partner matter (Jupiter rules your 4th and 7th) asks you to step back. Good for rest, bad for impulse buys.

8/10
By Ascendant (3/4)

Libra: Your 11th: income, friends, goals. A group or a side income changes shape. Your ruler Venus moves backward until 13 Nov: sign nothing big before then.

Scorpio: Your 10th: career, boss, reputation. A role stops meaning what it did. Your ruler Mars storms in on 12 Nov: be bold, but sleep on any resignation.

Sagittarius: Your own planet meets Ketu in your 9th: beliefs, teachers, father, long trips. A teacher or a belief gets tested. Keep the lesson, drop the idol.

9/10
By Ascendant (4/4)

Capricorn: Your 8th: shared money, taxes, insurance, sudden turns. Mercury moves backward through your career house until 13 Nov: review, don't resign.

Aquarius: Your 7th: partner, clients, contracts. Someone pulls back or a deal changes shape. Rahu sits on you, so you want more: listen before you push.

Pisces: Your own planet goes into your 6th with Ketu: work, health, debts. Good for clearing a loan or fixing a habit. Don't rush at work or the gym.

10/10
How to use it:
Until 13 Nov, finish things; don't sign or launch.
13 to 16 Nov, keep big decisions out of it; drive and speak with care.
After 5 Dec, keep what you've lived and let the rest go.

You cannot carry the old skin and sit straight at the same time.
