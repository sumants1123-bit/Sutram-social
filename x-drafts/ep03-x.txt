The Thread, Episode 3: The person of Time

An old hymn in the Rig Veda tells of a giant person whose body became the world: "The Moon was born from his mind, the Sun from his eye" (Rig Veda 10.90.13, the Purusha Sukta).

Jyotish turned that picture around. It put the whole sky inside one person and called him Kalapurusha, the person of Time (kala means time). His body is the twelve signs, Aries at the head down to Pisces at the feet (Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, chapter 4):
Aries: head
Taurus: face
Gemini: arms
Cancer: heart
Leo: belly
Virgo: waist
Libra: lower belly
Scorpio: groin
Sagittarius: thighs
Capricorn: knees
Aquarius: calves
Pisces: feet

His inner life is the grahas (Sanskrit for "the ones who grab"). Varahamihira gives the list in Brihat Jataka 2.1:
the Sun, the king: his soul
the Moon, the mother: his mind
Mars, the soldier: his strength
Mercury, the messenger: his speech
Jupiter, the teacher: his knowledge and happiness
Venus, the lover: his desire
Saturn, the auditor: his sorrow
Rahu and Ketu are not in this verse.

Your chart is a small copy of that person, so it works like a checklist. No energy? Go to the soldier: move first, think later. Words come out wrong? The messenger: write it, then send. A heavy week? The auditor: do one hard thing well.

Read it in your chart: open your free chart at sutram.ca/chart and look for a graha in its own sign. The Sun owns Leo; the Moon, Cancer; Mars, Aries and Scorpio; Mercury, Gemini and Virgo; Jupiter, Sagittarius and Pisces; Venus, Taurus and Libra; Saturn, Capricorn and Aquarius.
