Your dasha is the chapter. Transits are the weather
Most people check the daily stuff: Mercury retrograde, the Moon's sign, what Saturn is doing this month. Underneath that runs a bigger clock. Jyotish divides your life into planetary periods called dashas. In Parashara's main system, Vimshottari, the full cycle is 120 years, and each planet gets its own stretch, from 6 years for the Sun to 20 years for Venus.

The planet whose period you are in sets the theme of those years. Where that planet sits in your chart shows which part of life the theme plays out in.

For one person it's a Saturn period: 19 years of building slowly, a career that rewards patience more than talent
For another it's a Rahu period: 18 years of wanting more, new cities, new industries, big swings
For another it's a Jupiter period: 16 years of teachers, study, children and a wider circle

A transit is a few weeks or months of weather inside that chapter. A rainy week in a good chapter is still a good chapter. A sunny week in a hard chapter doesn't change the plot.

Sutra:
"Read the chapter before you blame the weather."

Practical remedy:
Find out which planet's period you are in, and which house that planet sits in. Write one line: "These years are about ___." Check every big decision against that line, not against today's horoscope.
