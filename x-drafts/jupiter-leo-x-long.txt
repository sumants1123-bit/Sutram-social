Jupiter walked into Leo on 31 October.

The teacher has walked into the king's palace. Read the Bhrigu Nandi Nadi way, here's who he meets inside, what changes, and what to do with it.

In Nadi, the grahas are characters. Graha is Sanskrit for "the one who grabs".

Jupiter is the teacher: wisdom, faith, mentors, savings, children. He is also jiva, the life in you: where he walks in your chart, your year grows.

Leo is the king's palace, owned by the Sun: the stage, leadership, kids and creativity.

Where he's coming from
Since June the teacher lived in Cancer, where he is strongest, working hand in hand with the auditor, Saturn in Pisces. Same element, so in Nadi they act as one. He walks into Leo carrying that homework, and the link holds: the auditor walks backward to face him until 10 Dec, the teacher looks back from 12 Dec, and they are in step again on 3 Apr and 11 Jul 2027.

Who's in the palace
The monk (Ketu) is already inside, in his own star. They meet exactly on 11 Nov.
The soldier (Mars) is tired and just behind. He walks in on 12 Nov, and from 13 to 16 Nov all three stand within a degree.
Across the room sit the magician (Rahu) and the auditor (Saturn), until early December.
The owner, the king (Sun), is away in Libra and weak.

What changes
Teachers and real experts take the stage.
Leaders get questioned: with the king away, bosses and governments face experts, courts and banks.
Hype gets audited: fake gurus and quick-money schemes get exposed by early December.
Kids and creative work come forward, mostly after 13 Nov.

16 Dec to 14 Jan: the king and the teacher swap houses. The Sun moves into Sagittarius, the teacher's home, while the teacher sits in the king's. A swap ties two grahas together like business partners: power listens to wisdom, mentors get authority, fathers and children make peace. Ask a mentor to vouch for you then.

24 Jan: back to Cancer to finish old lessons. 25 Jun 2027: he returns for the long stay, to 26 Nov.
