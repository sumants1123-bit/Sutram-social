A retrograde planet in your birth chart is a second draft
Right now Mercury, Venus and Saturn all appear to move backward in the sky. Some people were born with one or more planets in that state. It works in a particular way.

Nothing actually reverses. From Earth, a planet seems to move backward when we overtake it in orbit, or it overtakes us. At that time it is also at its closest to us. The classics count a retrograde planet as strong (Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, on planetary strength), and in real life that strength often shows up as going back and doing it better.

For one person it's retrograde Mercury: they rewrite every email and end up the best editor in the office
For another it's retrograde Venus: love and money choices get revisited, and the second choice is the one that lasts
For another it's retrograde Saturn: old duties come back to be finished properly, often later in life

Sutra:
"Some gifts arrive as second drafts."

Practical remedy:
Check if any planet in your chart is retrograde. In that area of life, stop expecting to get it right the first time. Build in a review: sleep on the email, wait a week on the purchase, look at the plan again in a month.
