A graha walking backward in your birth chart is a second draft

Picture someone walking home who looks back, sees a person drowning, changes path and goes to save them. That is retrograde: a graha (Sanskrit for "the one who grabs", one of the nine forces in your chart) that turns around and goes back for what was missed.

Right now the messenger (Mercury), the lover (Venus) and the auditor (Saturn) all appear to walk backward in the sky. Some people were born with one or more grahas in that state.

Nothing actually reverses. From Earth, a graha seems to move backward when we overtake it in orbit, or it overtakes us. At that time it is also at its closest to us. The classics count a retrograde graha as strong (Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, on strength), and in real life that strength often shows up as going back and doing it better.

For one person it's the messenger walking backward: they rewrite every email and end up the best editor in the office
For another it's the lover walking backward: love and money choices get revisited, and the second choice is the one that lasts
For another it's the auditor walking backward: old duties come back to be finished properly, often later in life

Sutra:
"Some gifts arrive as second drafts."

Practical remedy:
Check if any graha in your chart is retrograde. In that area of life, stop expecting to get it right the first time. Build in a review: sleep on the email, wait a week on the purchase, look at the plan again in a month.
