1/7
As above, so below.

The sky over us right now has happened only six times since 1600. We checked every day with our engine. Here's what happened on the ground each time.

2/7
The line comes from the Emerald Tablet, an Arabic text from about 800 CE credited to Hermes: "That which is above is like to that which is below."

India said it too. Charaka: "puruṣo'yaṃ lokasammitaḥ", a person is a measure of the world (Sharira 5.3). And Varahamihira read the sky for whole countries: rain, harvests, famine, war.

3/7
The sky now: Jupiter, the teacher, meets Ketu, the monk, in Leo, the king's palace. Graha is Sanskrit for "the one who grabs".

Since 1600 this meeting has happened in 1635, 1672, 1766, 1802, 1896 and 1932. Now 2026.

4/7
October 1635, almost this exact sky (Mars close by, the Sun weak): Massachusetts banishes a preacher, Roger Williams, for his beliefs. He founds Rhode Island on freedom of conscience.

June 1672, Saturn in Pisces like now: France invades the Dutch Republic. The Dutch still call it the disaster year.

5/7
August 1896, the same degree as this November: a man takes Zanzibar's palace without approval. The war that follows lasts about 38 minutes, the shortest on record. Famine and plague hit India.

Autumn 1932: Gandhi fasts in prison until the Poona Pact. Famine spreads in Ukraine. The Depression hits bottom. America changes leader.

6/7
What repeats: conscience against power. The palace contested. Sudden strikes. Hardship, then new freedoms.

What to watch, Nov to Jan: people of conscience stepping out, thrones tested, fights that flare fast (12 to 16 Nov, when Mars joins), food and money worries in places.

7/7
So within: stand by what you've lived. Don't take a seat you weren't offered. Pick short, clean fights. Keep a small reserve this winter.

When the sky rhymes, history rhymes.
