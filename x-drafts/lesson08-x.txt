Lesson 8: Saturn, the auditor

In Jyotish each graha (Sanskrit for "the one who grabs") is a character in your life's story. Saturn is the auditor: work, routine, time and patience. He checks what you built and how you built it. Varahamihira calls Saturn the sorrow of Time (Brihat Jataka 2.1): the slow, hard lessons that make you grow up. Mantreswara puts long life and servants under it (Phaladeepika, chapter 2). His Sanskrit name is Shani, short for Shanaishchara, "the one who moves slowly", because he takes about 30 years to go once around the zodiac.

Picture two friends. One keeps her routines, takes the slow job seriously, and ten years later everyone trusts her with the keys. That's strong Saturn. The other waits for the perfect moment, delays pile up, and every shortcut costs her later. That's weak Saturn. Too much Saturn is the friend who can't take a day off.

In Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, Saturn is strongest in Libra (exalted), at home in Capricorn and Aquarius (its own signs) and weakest in Aries (debilitated). Right now the auditor sits in Pisces and is walking backward until 10 December, checking old work before he moves on. He stays in Pisces until June 2027.

The Bhagavad Gita says: "Your right is to the work alone, never to its fruits" (2.47). Do the work, and let the results take their own time. In your chart, Saturn shows where patience pays.
