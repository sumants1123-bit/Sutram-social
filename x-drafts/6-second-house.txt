Your words and your savings live in the same house
In Jyotish the 2nd house covers your money, your speech, your family and your food. It sounds like a random mix until you watch how real people live.

For one person it's the family dinner table: the way money was talked about there becomes the way they handle it for life
For another it's speech at work: the person who can say "this is my rate" without apologising usually earns more
For another it's food: the daily takeout and coffee runs that quietly decide whether anything is left to save

The old astrologers put these under one house because they saw one system. What goes into your mouth, and what comes out of it, shapes what you keep.

Sutra:
"What you say about money becomes what you do with it."

A strong 2nd house often gives a voice people trust, a steady saver, and a family that stays close around meals.

Practical remedy:
For one week, catch every sentence you say about money ("I'm bad with money", "we can never afford it"). Swap one for a plain fact ("I have $400 left this month"). And cook at home three nights. Write down what you saved.
