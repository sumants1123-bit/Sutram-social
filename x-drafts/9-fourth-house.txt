Your peace has an address
In Jyotish the 4th house covers your home, your mother, your vehicle and something the old texts call sukha: inner ease, the feeling of being settled.

They share a house because they answer one question: where do you feel safe enough to rest?
For one person it's their mother's kitchen, even over a video call
For another it's the car after work: twenty quiet minutes in the parking lot before going inside
For another it's one corner of the apartment with good light and no laptop

Parashara calls the 4th house sukha, happiness (Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra). In city life, with long commutes and small apartments, it's usually the first house to get crowded out.

Sutra:
"Your peace has an address. Visit it every day."

A strong 4th house makes people who can turn any rented room into a home, and who make others feel at home too.

Practical remedy:
Pick one spot in your home that is only for rest: no work, no bills, no scrolling. Spend ten minutes there every day. If you live with family, tell them it's your spot.
