Lesson 7: Venus, the lover

In Jyotish each graha (Sanskrit for "the one who grabs") is a character in your life's story. Venus is the lover: love and partnership, beauty, art, music, and the comforts you enjoy. Varahamihira calls Venus the desire of Time (Brihat Jataka 2.1), and Mantreswara puts marriage, vehicles, jewellery, perfume and music under it (Phaladeepika, chapter 2). His Sanskrit name is Shukra, "the bright one".

Picture two friends. One makes any room nicer, people relax around her, and love feels easy. That's strong Venus. The other holds back: love feels like work, every treat comes with guilt, and she can't enjoy what she already has. That's weak Venus. Too much Venus is the friend who can't skip a treat.

In Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, Venus is strongest in Pisces (exalted), at home in Taurus and Libra (its own signs) and weakest in Virgo (debilitated). Right now the lover is walking backward. On 5 November he slips back into Virgo, turns forward there on 13 November, and returns to Libra on 22 November. A good few weeks to repair what you already love before you buy something new.

In the Bhagavad Gita, Krishna says: "In every being I am desire that keeps to what is right" (7.11). Desire is welcome when it keeps to your values. In your chart, Venus shows what you desire.
