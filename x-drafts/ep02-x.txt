The Thread, Episode 2: Five layers of you

An old Indian text tells how a young sage named Bhrigu asked his father, Varuna, to teach him what is real. His father gave him a clue: find what everything is born from, lives by and returns to. Bhrigu went away and worked on it (Taittiriya Upanishad, chapter 3). Each time he came back with an answer, and each answer went one layer deeper:

Food: the body you feed
Breath: your energy and drive
Mind: your feelings and moods
Knowing: your judgment and understanding
Joy: the deepest layer

Later teachers called these the five koshas, "sheaths", one inside the next.

In your chart, each layer has a graha (Sanskrit for "the one who grabs") looking after it. This is Sutram's teaching map, built from the classical meanings of each one:
Body: your Ascendant, the sign rising when you were born
Breath: the Sun, the king, for vitality, and Mars, the soldier, for drive
Mind: the Moon, the mother
Knowing: Mercury, the messenger, and Jupiter, the teacher
Joy: Venus, the lover, and Jupiter again

In real life it works like a checklist. Tired all week? Start with the body: sleep, food, a walk. Can't get going? The breath layer: morning light and movement. Can't switch off? The mind layer: a routine, a call home.

Bhrigu's last answer was joy: "From joy all beings are born, by joy they live, into joy they return" (Taittiriya Upanishad 3.6). Jyotish still carries his name too: the Bhrigu Samhita and Bhrigu Nandi Nadi are named for a sage Bhrigu.
