Five questions to ask your chart this November

November's sky is busy. Each graha (Sanskrit for "the one who grabs", the nine characters in your chart) that moves this month lands in a different part of your life. Here are five questions worth asking, and where to ask them.

1. "Which part of my life does the teacher walk into?" Jupiter, the teacher, entered Leo on 31 October. Wherever Leo sits in your chart gets a year of growth, mentors and savings. This first visit lasts until 24 January.

2. "Should I sign now, or after the 13th?" Mercury, the messenger, is walking backward until 13 November. Contracts, launches and big messages go better at the right time.

3. "What's going on with my love life and spending?" Venus, the lover, slips back into Virgo, his weakest sign, from 5 to 22 November, and turns forward on the 13th.

4. "Where should I push, and where should I pause?" Mars, the soldier, walks into Leo on 12 November. From 13 to 16 November he, Jupiter and Ketu, the monk, stand within a degree of each other.

5. "Which dasha am I in, and what is it about?" A dasha is the stretch of years one graha runs. Transits are the weather; your dasha is the chapter.
