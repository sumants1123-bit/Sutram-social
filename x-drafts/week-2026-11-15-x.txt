Week ahead: the king gets stronger

On Monday morning the Sun moves from Libra into Scorpio. In Jyotish the Sun is the king, and Libra is the sign where he is weakest (Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, chapter 3), so this is the king walking out of the wrong room. He stays in Scorpio until mid-December.

To see what it means for you, count from your Moon sign, the sign the Moon was in when you were born. The classics give the Sun his best results in the 3rd, 6th, 10th and 11th from the Moon (Phaladeepika, chapter 26).

Aries Moon, 8th: a quiet month; mind your health
Taurus Moon, 7th: partner and clients; drop the ego
Gemini Moon, 6th: beat deadlines and rivals
Cancer Moon, 5th: kids, study, romance; less stress
Leo Moon, 4th: home and mother; rest more
Virgo Moon, 3rd: courage; make the pitch
Libra Moon, 2nd: money talks; watch your words
Scorpio Moon, 1st: you in focus; don't overheat
Sagittarius Moon, 12th: spending, travel, sleep
Capricorn Moon, 11th: gains and good friends
Aquarius Moon, 10th: career push; the boss notices
Pisces Moon, 9th: father, teachers, long trips

Also this week: early on Monday the soldier (Mars) passes the teacher (Jupiter) in Leo. On Friday the mother (Moon) meets the auditor (Saturn) in Pisces: a good evening to finish one chore. On Sunday the lover (Venus) walks home into Libra, her own sign.

The Katha Upanishad says: "When he shines, everything shines after him" (2.2.15). The line is about the Self, the light behind every light. In your chart, the Sun shows where you shine.
