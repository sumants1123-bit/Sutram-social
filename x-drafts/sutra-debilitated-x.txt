Debilitated grahas: a skill learned the long way

Three parts: the idea, today's sky, your chart

PART 1 · THE IDEA
In Jyotish every graha (Sanskrit for "the one who grabs") has one sign where it is at its best, exalted, and one where it is at its weakest, called neecha, "low" (Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, chapter 3). The two signs sit straight across from each other. Think of the weakest sign as the wrong room: the graha is still there, it just can't work the way it likes.

The weakest signs:
the king (Sun): Libra
the mother (Moon): Scorpio
the soldier (Mars): Cancer
the messenger (Mercury): Pisces
the teacher (Jupiter): Capricorn
the lover (Venus): Virgo
the auditor (Saturn): Aries

The old texts also give ways the fall gets cancelled, called neecha bhanga. One of them: the owner of that sign sits in the 1st, 4th, 7th or 10th house from your rising sign or your Moon (Phaladeepika 7.26). Then the weak spot often turns into a hard-won skill: the shy kid who becomes the speaker, the person who grew up broke and gets great with money.

PART 2 · TODAY'S SKY
Two grahas sit in the wrong room right now. The lover (Venus) is in Virgo from 5 to 22 November. The king (Sun) is in Libra until Monday morning, 16 November. Read with the rule, pleasure is harder to enjoy and pride bruises easily. Go easy on spending and on ego fights this weekend.

PART 3 · YOUR CHART
Born between about 17 October and 15 November? Your king (Sun) is in Libra, his weakest sign. For the other six grahas, open your chart, look for one in its weakest sign, then check the way out.

Sutra: "A skill learned the long way is still a skill"

Practical remedy: name the skill your weakest graha holds, then practice it on purpose for ten minutes a day
