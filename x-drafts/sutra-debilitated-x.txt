Debilitated grahas: a skill learned the long way

In Jyotish every graha (Sanskrit for "the one who grabs") has one sign where it is at its best, exalted, and one where it is at its weakest, called neecha, "low" (Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, chapter 3). Think of it as the wrong room: the graha is still there, it just can't work the way it likes.

The weakest signs:
the king (Sun): Libra
the mother (Moon): Scorpio
the soldier (Mars): Cancer
the messenger (Mercury): Pisces
the teacher (Jupiter): Capricorn
the lover (Venus): Virgo
the auditor (Saturn): Aries

The old texts also give ways the fall gets cancelled, called neecha bhanga. One of them: the owner of that sign sits in the 1st, 4th, 7th or 10th house from your Ascendant or your Moon (Phaladeepika 7.26). Then the weak spot often turns into a hard-won skill: the shy kid who becomes the speaker, the person who grew up broke and gets great with money.

Right now two grahas sit in the wrong room: the king (Sun) in Libra until Monday morning, and the lover (Venus) in Virgo until 22 November. Go easy on pride and on spending this weekend.

Sutra: "A skill learned the long way is still a skill"

Practical remedy: find any graha in its weakest sign in your chart. Name the skill it holds and practice it on purpose, ten minutes a day.
