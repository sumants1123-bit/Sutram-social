# The Thread: "Maps of you" (approved by Sumant, 7 Nov 2026)

Sumant loved "Five layers of you" (Ep 2, Bhrigu and the koshas) and approved all ten ideas below. The point, in his words: it's about you, not about planets. Each episode takes one old map of a human being and lays the grahas over it.

## The shape of every episode (same as Ep 2)
1. An old story, told as a scene, with a source.
2. The map of you: the layers, strands or stages, each with its graha. Say "This is Sutram's teaching map, built from the classical meanings" whenever the pairing is ours rather than the text's.
3. A real-life checklist: three lines such as "Tired all week? Start with the body."
4. One quote, our own rendering or public domain, with its source.
5. NEW last content slide, "Read it in your chart": one concrete step and the free tool on sutram.ca that shows it.
6. The question for the comments, then the two links (asksutram.com first, then sutram.ca/newsletter).
Slides stay very simple with big text. Graha characters as always: Sun the king, Moon the mother, Mars the soldier, Mercury the messenger, Jupiter the teacher, Venus the lover, Saturn the auditor, Rahu the magician, Ketu the monk.

### Ep 4, Wed 18 Nov: The three strands
- Story: the Gita, chapter 14. Krishna tells Arjuna that three gunas, born of nature, bind the one who lives in the body (14.5). Guna literally means a strand of a rope, so it fits Sutram, the thread.
- Map (classical: Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, chapter 3): clear (sattva) = Sun, Moon, Jupiter; restless (rajas) = Mercury, Venus; heavy (tamas) = Mars, Saturn.
- Checklist: restless, so do one thing at a time and walk; heavy, so get light, move and start small; clear, so use it to study and decide.
- Read it in your chart: find your lagna lord and Moon on sutram.ca/chart. Which strand do they belong to?
- Question: which strand runs strongest in you?

### Ep 5, Wed 25 Nov: Two birds on one tree
- Story: Mundaka Upanishad 3.1.1-2 (first in Rig Veda 1.164.20). Two birds sit on one tree. One eats the fruit, sweet and bitter. The other doesn't eat; it watches. The eating bird grieves until it sees the watching one, and then its sorrow goes.
- Map (Sutram's teaching map): the Moon is the bird that eats (the mind tasting life); the Sun is the bird that watches (the Self, "soul of Time" in Brihat Jataka 2.1). The tithi you were born on is the distance between them: on a new Moon the two birds sit together; on a full Moon the mind is as far as it goes, and fully lit. Tie-in: full Moon on 24 Nov (Kartik Purnima).
- Checklist: caught in craving or scrolling? Take one breath and notice who is watching.
- Read it in your chart: ask which tithi you were born on at asksutram.com.
- Question: are you more the eating bird or the watching one this week?

### Ep 6, Wed 2 Dec: Your body type
- Story: Ayurveda's three doshas: vata (air and space: movement, dryness, worry), pitta (fire: heat, drive, temper), kapha (earth and water: steadiness, weight, calm).
- Map (classical: Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, chapter 3): Sun pitta; Moon vata and kapha; Mars pitta; Mercury all three; Jupiter kapha; Venus kapha and vata; Saturn vata.
- Checklist for winter, which is vata season: warm food, a fixed bedtime, oil on dry skin. One line saying this is not medical advice.
- Read it in your chart: your lagna lord and Moon on sutram.ca/chart. Which dosha do they lean to?
- Question: which one sounds like you?

### Ep 7, Wed 9 Dec: Nachiketa and Death
- Story: Katha Upanishad, part 1. A boy, Nachiketa, waits three nights at Death's door. Yama, the Lord of Death, offers him wealth, long life and pleasures instead of the secret of death. The boy refuses. Yama tells him: the good and the pleasant both come to every person; the wise choose the good (1.2.1-2).
- Chart link: Yama is the son of the Sun, and so is Saturn: they are brothers. Saturn turns forward on 10 Dec.
- Map (Sutram's teaching map): Saturn = where life makes you wait at the door; Rahu = the pleasant offers, the magician's gifts; Ketu = the boy's calm refusal.
- Checklist: before a choice this week, ask "good or pleasant?"
- Read it in your chart: Saturn's house (where you wait) and Rahu's house (where shortcuts tempt you), on sutram.ca/chart.
- Question: what is your "pleasant" offer right now?

### Ep 8, Wed 16 Dec: The four aims of life
- Idea: Indian thought gives life four aims (purusharthas): dharma (your duty and purpose), artha (work and money), kama (love and enjoyment), moksha (freedom).
- Map (standard Jyotish teaching): houses 1, 5, 9 = dharma; 2, 6, 10 = artha; 3, 7, 11 = kama; 4, 8, 12 = moksha.
- Checklist: a good week touches all four. Which one did you skip?
- Read it in your chart: count your grahas in each group on sutram.ca/chart.
- Question: where does your chart lean?

### Ep 9, Wed 23 Dec: Three bodies
- Idea: Tattva Bodha (attributed to Shankara) says you have three bodies: the gross body made of food, the subtle body (mind, intellect, senses, breath) and the causal body (the seeds of habit you carry).
- Map (Sutram's teaching map): gross = lagna and its lord; subtle = Moon and Mercury; causal = Ketu and the 12th house. Tie-in: the winter solstice, 21 Dec.
- Checklist: tired body, so food and sleep; tired mind, so quiet; the same pattern again, so look at the seed.
- Read it in your chart: your rising sign and Moon on sutram.ca/big-three.
- Question: which body needs care this week?

### Ep 10, Wed 30 Dec: The four stages of life
- Idea: the four ashramas: student, householder, stepping back (vanaprastha, "the forest-dweller"), letting go (sannyasa).
- Map (Sutram's teaching map): Jupiter returns every 12 years (12, 24, 36, 48, 60); Saturn returns at about 29 and 59. The first Saturn return is when the householder years begin for real; the second is when stepping back begins.
- Checklist: which stage are you living, and which does your age say?
- Read it in your chart: ask where Saturn stands from your birth Saturn at asksutram.com.
- Question: which stage are you in?

### Ep 11, Wed 6 Jan: Waking, dreaming, deep sleep and the fourth
- Idea: Mandukya Upanishad: the waking self, the dreaming self, the self in deep sleep, and the fourth (turiya), the one present in all three.
- Map (Sutram's teaching map): Sun = waking (the world lit up); Moon = dreaming (the mind's own light); 12th house = deep sleep; the fourth = you, the witness, which no graha holds.
- Checklist: before sleep tonight, notice the moment between waking and dreaming.
- Read it in your chart: your Sun and Moon on sutram.ca/big-three.
- Question: which of the four do you know best?

### Ep 13, Wed 20 Jan: The three paths
- Idea: the Gita's three paths: action (karma yoga, chapter 3), devotion (bhakti yoga, chapter 12), knowledge (jnana yoga, chapters 4 and 13).
- Map (Sutram's teaching map): action = Saturn, Mars and the 10th house; devotion = Moon, Venus and the 4th house; knowledge = Mercury, Jupiter, Ketu and the 5th and 9th houses.
- Checklist: the path that feels easy is your door; the others still count.
- Read it in your chart: which group holds your strongest graha, on sutram.ca/chart.
- Question: which path is yours?
