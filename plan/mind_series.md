## Posts
0. Seven kinds of mind. Made 19 Nov, approved in content, waiting for asksutram.com/mind to go live.
1. Five kinds of thought. Yoga Sutra 1.5 to 1.11: every thought is right knowing, wrong knowing, imagining, sleep or memory.
   Real life: catch which kind the last hour was made of.
2. Nine kinds of feeling. The nine rasas of the Natya Shastra: love, laughter, sorrow, anger, courage, fear, disgust, wonder, peace.
   Which graha carries which feeling is Sutram's reading; BPHS 3.12 to 3.13 names what each graha stands for.
3. Who is driving? Katha Upanishad 1.3.3 to 1.3.4: the self rides, the intellect drives, the mind is the reins, the senses are the horses.
4. The player and the watcher. Mundaka Upanishad 3.1.1: two birds on one tree, one eats the fruit, one watches.
   Sumant's words: "We are just like observer and the player in that."
5. Why you repeat yourself. Yoga Sutra 2.12 to 2.13 on stored impressions, and the dasha clock that starts from the Moon's star (BPHS ch. 46).
6. Three weathers of the mind. Gita ch. 14: sattva, rajas, tamas. BPHS ch. 3 gives each graha one of the three.
7. Your mind's 27-day rhythm. The Moon returns to your star every 27 days; nine kinds of days in between. Ends at Today on asksutram.com.
