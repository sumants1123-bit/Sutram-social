Lesson 15: Bhrigu Nandi Nadi, rule 5: looking back

Some days a graha (Sanskrit for "the one who grabs") seems to walk backward through the sky. Jyotish calls it vakri, retrograde.

Rule 5 says a graha walking backward works from its own sign and from the sign behind it too.

Picture yourself walking down the street. You hear a shout, turn, and see someone in the water, so you go back to help. That's a graha walking backward: old business pulls it back before it moves on.

Today two of them turn forward, Toronto time: the messenger (Mercury) at 10:55 AM in Libra, and the lover (Venus) at 7:30 PM in Virgo.

Now use the rule on this week's sky. The sign behind Virgo is Leo. So all week, walking backward in Virgo, the lover also worked from Leo, the room of the teacher (Jupiter), the monk (Ketu) and now the soldier (Mars). Old loves and old money questions sat in that room. Tonight the lover turns and walks on.

In real life, think of the ex who texted, the refund you chased, the price you kept rechecking. From tonight those start to settle. Old talks restart too, now that the messenger moves forward. The auditor (Saturn) keeps looking back in Pisces until 10 December, so unfinished work still asks to be finished.

A real case. On 23 August 2023 India's Chandrayaan-3 landed near the Moon's south pole, four years after its first lander crashed. Look at the sky that evening. The auditor was walking backward in Aquarius, so old work was back on the table. Straight across, in Leo, sat the king (the Sun) and the messenger: a country tested, live on air. Rule 5 and rule 4 together read as going back to finish an old job with everyone watching. The sky set the scene. The engineers did the work.

In your chart, find any graha marked R for retrograde. Read it in its own sign and in the sign behind it.
