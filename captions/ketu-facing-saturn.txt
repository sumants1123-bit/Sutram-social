How does a transit turn into an event?

Ketu moves into Cancer and Rahu into Capricorn on Saturday 5 December (by the true path it came on 25 November), and they stay until June 2028.

In an example chart, Saturn sits in Capricorn, the 2nd house of savings. Ketu in Cancer, the 8th, looks across at it: every graha looks at the 7th sign from itself (Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra 26.4). Sutram reads the 8th as sudden turns, hidden matters and shared money, which reach the savings.

On Saturn or facing it? Ketu on a graha pauses its karma or brings obstacles (Jitender Kumar, 100 Nadi Sutras, sutra 33). From the 7th it works at about half strength (his Nadi Jyotisha, chapter 3). And Ketu never faces Saturn alone: Rahu sits on Saturn at the same time.

Two aspects land on Ketu. Saturn's 7th: quick to arrive, slow to settle. Mars's special 4th (also 26.4): sharper, and costly here, since Mars rules the 12th, the house of spending (11.13).

Ketu has no sign of its own. The nodes give results through the house they hold and the lords they join (34.16). Sutram follows Ketu's sign lord, here the Moon in the 11th house of gains: what goes out can come back.

An event needs four parts: the birth chart makes the promise, aspects link it, the running dasha (your graha period) switches it on, and the exact degree sparks it. In this chart Rahu reaches Saturn's degree on 17 March 2027.

Nadi drops houses and special aspects. Grahas link only by direction, so Mars and the Moon fall out. R. G. Rao reads Ketu with Saturn as breaks in the job (Fundamentals of Rao's System of Nadi Astrology, chapter 21). The story: one form of work ends, a bigger one grows, money moves both ways.

Your own chart:
1. Find the houses of Cancer and Capricorn from your rising sign
2. See which grahas sit there, or 7th from them
3. Note the day Rahu or Ketu reaches each one's degree

It is not loss for everyone. Your dasha and whole chart set how loud it gets.
