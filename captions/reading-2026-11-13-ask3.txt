Read this weekend's sky with five Nadi rules

Four parts: the sky, rule by rule, the reading, your chart

PART 1 · THE SKY
Friday 13 to Sunday 15 November, Toronto time. Leo holds three grahas (Sanskrit for "the ones who grab"), less than one degree apart: the soldier (Mars), the monk (Ketu) and the teacher (Jupiter).

PART 2 · RULE BY RULE
Rule 1, same room. The three in Leo act as one.
Rule 3, more degrees lead. The teacher leads for now. The soldier walks fastest: he passes the monk on Saturday and the teacher on Monday.
Rule 2, ahead and behind. Cancer, behind Leo, is empty. Ahead, the lover (Venus) turns forward tonight.
Rule 4, face to face. The magician (Rahu) sits in Aquarius, straight across from Leo.
Rule 5, looking back. The auditor (Saturn) walks backward in Pisces, so he also sits with the magician.

PART 3 · THE READING
Tempers run short around big egos. Bold claims get checked against old records. An old message or an old flame returns. Slow answers win.

PART 4 · YOUR CHART
Find Leo in your chart: slides 7 and 8 list it for every rising sign. Write down what you expect and check it on Monday.
