Lesson 9: Rahu, the magician

In Jyotish each graha (Sanskrit for "the one who grabs") is a character in your life's story. Rahu is the magician: hunger for more, the new and the foreign, and smoke and mirrors. You can't see Rahu in the sky. He is the point where the Moon's path crosses the Sun's, and eclipses happen near him.

Strong Rahu breaks into new fields and rises fast, often through foreign places or new technology. Weak Rahu chases: never enough, shortcuts, then a crash. Too much Rahu is the friend who is always on to the next thing.

Right now Rahu is in Aquarius until 5 December, then moves into Capricorn until June 2028. The house he crosses in your chart is where you feel the hunger.
