Lesson 2: the Sun

In Jyotish the Sun is the self you shine from. Varahamihira puts it first: the Sun is the soul of Time, the Moon its mind (Brihat Jataka 2.1). It shows your purpose and confidence, your father and the people in authority over you, and your basic health and vitality.

The Sun owns Leo, is exalted in Aries and debilitated in Libra (Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, on the planets). Its friends are the Moon, Mars and Jupiter. Its day is Sunday, its element fire, its direction east, its gem the ruby.

Where it sits matters as much as its sign. Parashara gives the Sun full directional strength in the 10th house, the house of career and standing, so a 10th house Sun tends to live a visible life. In the 3rd, 6th and 11th it competes well and wins with effort. In the 8th and 12th its light turns inward.

The Upanishads call the Self "the pure light of all lights" (Mundaka Upanishad 2.2.9). The Sun in your chart is the closest symbol Jyotish has for that light.
