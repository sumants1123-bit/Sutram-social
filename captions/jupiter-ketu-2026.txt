Jupiter meets Ketu, and Mars arrives

On 31 October Jupiter crosses from Cancer into Leo, through a knot point the classics call Gandanta, where a water sign meets a fire sign (Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra). It walks into Magha, Ketu's own star, whose deity is the ancestors, and meets Ketu: exact on 11 to 12 November. Mars follows through the same doorway on 12 November, and from 13 to 16 November all three are within one degree. Mercury and Venus both turn forward on 13 November, inside that window.

Read it the way Bhrigu Nandi Nadi reads a chart, as a story. Jupiter is the teacher: what you were taught, your faith, your savings. Ketu is the monk: what you already know, and what you're done with. Mars is the soldier: the push to act. Together they test what you only borrowed against what you actually lived. Stuck decisions move fast, people quit or speak up for what they believe, and leaders and institutions face a test. Drive, cook and speak with care that week.

On 5 December Ketu slips back into Cancer, Saturn turns forward on 10 December and Jupiter pauses on 12 December. In 2027 Jupiter meets Ketu twice more, about 1 March and 19 April. November is the first lesson.
