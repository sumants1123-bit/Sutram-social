Know yourself

It was carved at the entrance to the Temple of Apollo at Delphi, and every tradition gives it first. The Upanishads say, "Know that one Self, and leave other words." The Tao Te Ching says, "He who knows himself is intelligent." The Sufis say, "Whoever knows himself knows his Lord." Socrates built his life on it: the unexamined life is not worth living.

Philosophy gives us the question. Your birth chart gives the personal detail: your body, your drive, your mind, how you think, what brings you joy, and when each of these comes forward in your life.

This is The Thread, Sutram's weekly series. Every Wednesday: one idea from a wisdom tradition, where it shows up in your chart, and what your own chart says about it.

Start here. Open your chart and find three things:
Your Ascendant: the body, and how you meet the world
Your Moon: the mind, and how you feel
Your Sun: the soul, and what you're here for
Parashara taught reading a chart from all three.
