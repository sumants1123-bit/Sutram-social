The rose of Venus

On 23 October, Venus passed between the Earth and the Sun. It does this every 584 days, and five of these passes take almost exactly eight years. So the pattern repeats.

Follow Venus from the Earth every day for eight years and its path draws a five-petal rose. The five passes form a small star at the centre, and this latest one landed almost where the October 2018 pass did. Every point here comes from real positions, computed in the Sutram engine.

In Jyotish, Venus is Shukra, the planet of love, beauty and comfort. Varahamihira calls it the desire of Time (Brihat Jataka 2.1), and in the Puranas Shukra is the teacher of the asuras.

Venus is moving backward now and turns forward on 13 November. It's a good time to look again at what you love, and what you spend on.
