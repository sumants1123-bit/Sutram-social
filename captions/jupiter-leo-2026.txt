Jupiter in Leo: the teacher walks into the king's palace

Early today Jupiter left Cancer for Leo, the Sun's sign. Read it the Bhrigu Nandi Nadi way, where every graha (Sanskrit for "the one who grabs") is a character. Jupiter is the teacher: wisdom, faith, mentors, savings, children. In Nadi he is also jiva, the life in you, so the room he walks into is where your year grows. Leo is the king's palace: the stage, leadership, children, creativity and the heart.

Since June the teacher lived in Cancer, where he is strongest, working hand in hand with the auditor (Saturn in Pisces). Same element, so in Nadi they act as one. He carries that homework into Leo, and the two stay linked into 2027.

Who's in the palace: the monk (Ketu) is already inside, in his own star. The soldier (Mars) walks in on 12 November, and from 13 to 16 November all three stand within a degree. The king (Sun) is away in Libra and weak. Facing them across the room sit the magician (Rahu) and the auditor (Saturn), until early December.

What changes: teachers and real experts take the stage. Leaders get questioned. Hype and quick-money schemes get audited. Kids and creative work come forward. From 16 December to 14 January the king and the teacher swap houses, so power listens to wisdom.

Key dates: 11 Nov he meets the monk. 13 Nov Mercury and Venus walk forward again. 12 Dec he stops and looks back. 24 Jan he returns to Cancer to finish old lessons. 25 Jun 2027 he comes back for the long stay, to 26 Nov.
