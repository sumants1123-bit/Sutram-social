Today's Sky, Sunday 1 November

The Moon is in Pushya, in Cancer. Pushya means "the nourisher", and its deity is Brihaspati, teacher of the gods (the Vedic star list in the Taittiriya Brahmana). It's one of the swift stars, which the timing texts keep for learning, trade, medicine and skilled work (Muhurta Chintamani). Weddings are the one thing tradition keeps away from it.

In real life: cook for your family, start the course you keep putting off, check on someone who needs care. The Moon is in its own sign, so home feels good today.

Many mothers keep the Ahoi Ashtami fast today for their children, and break it when the first stars come out.

Sunday is the Sun's day, and the first morning back on standard time. The messenger (Mercury), the lover (Venus) and the auditor (Saturn) are still walking backward.

Good for: learning, cooking, family
Go slow on: signing and big buys
Rahu Kaal, a poor time to start anything: 3:50 to 5:06 PM Toronto
