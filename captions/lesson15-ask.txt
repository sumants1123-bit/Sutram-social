Lesson 15: Bhrigu Nandi Nadi, rule 5: looking back

Some days a graha (Sanskrit for "the one who grabs") seems to walk backward through the sky. Jyotish calls it vakri, retrograde.

Rule 5 says a graha walking backward works from its own sign and from the sign behind it too.

Picture yourself walking down the street. You hear a shout, turn, and see someone in the water, so you go back to help. That's a graha walking backward: old business pulls it back before it moves on.

Today two of them turn forward, Toronto time: the messenger (Mercury) at 10:55 AM in Libra, and the lover (Venus) at 7:30 PM in Virgo. This past week, walking backward in Virgo, the lover also worked from Leo, the room of the teacher (Jupiter) and the monk (Ketu). Old talks and old loves can start moving again. The auditor (Saturn) keeps looking back in Pisces until 10 December.

In your chart, find any graha marked R for retrograde. Read it in its own sign and in the sign behind it.
