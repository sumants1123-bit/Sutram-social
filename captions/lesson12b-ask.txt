Lesson 12, part 2: read tonight's sky with two rules

Yesterday and today gave you two Nadi rules. Rule 1 says grahas (Sanskrit for "the ones who grab") in one sign share a room. Rule 2 says the sign behind a graha is what it carries, and the sign ahead is where it is heading. Here is how to join them into one reading.

Step 1. Pick one graha. Start with the teacher (Jupiter), who stands for advice, growth and mentors. Tonight he is in Leo, the sign of the stage.

Step 2. Who shares his room? The monk (Ketu). The monk lets go and says little, so advice turns quiet and honest.

Step 3. Who is behind him? The soldier (Mars), in Cancer, his weakest sign. So the teacher carries a tired, old fight.

Step 4. Who is ahead? The lover (Venus), in Virgo, the lover's weakest sign. So he is heading toward a check on money and love.

Step 5. Join them. A quiet teacher carries an old fight toward a money check.

In real life this week, the best advice is short. An old argument at home runs out of fuel. Before you pay or say yes, you check twice.

Now make it yours. Find Leo in your chart and note its house. Scorpio rising? Leo is your 10th house, career. So a boss or mentor says less, an old conflict at work winds down, and a pay or budget question gets a second look. The last slides list all twelve rising signs.

On Thursday the soldier walks into Leo, and the sentence changes.
