Today's sky, Wednesday 28 October

The Moon is in Rohini, in Taurus. Taurus is the Moon's exaltation, the sign where it's strongest (Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, on the planets' dignities). Rohini is also the star the Moon loved most. The 27 stars were Daksha's daughters, all married to the Moon, and the Moon favoured Rohini. Daksha cursed him to waste away, the curse was softened, and the Moon has waned and waxed ever since (the Mahabharata, Shalya Parva).

The muhurta texts count Rohini among the fixed stars, the ones chosen for things meant to last: planting, building, laying foundations, steady work (Muhurta Chintamani).

It's the third day of the waning Moon, and Wednesday belongs to Mercury, which is retrograde in Libra until 13 November. So tend what you already have, and give new agreements another look before you sign.

Good for: steady work, planting, cooking, beauty, family
Go slow on: new contracts and launches
