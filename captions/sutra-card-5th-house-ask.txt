The 5th house shows what came to you easily

In Jyotish the 5th house covers children, creativity, intelligence, and what the old texts call purva punya: the good you carry in from before, often read as merit from past lives.

You can test it without believing in past lives. Look at what you were good at before anyone taught you.

For one person it's numbers: they split the restaurant bill in their head at age 12
For another it's people: kids, students and juniors simply listen to them
For another it's making things: cooking, drawing, writing or music, without a single lesson

Some talent arrives already paid for. What you do with it is up to you. A strong 5th house often makes good teachers, parents, creators and advisors.

Sutra: "What comes easily to you was earned somewhere. Spend it on someone"

Practical remedy: list three things you do well without effort. Pick one and use it for someone else this week: help a nephew with math, cook for a friend who's had a hard week, review a junior's CV.
