Lesson 10: Ketu, the monk

In Jyotish each graha (Sanskrit for "the one who grabs") is a character in your life's story. Ketu is the monk: letting go, skills you were born with, and the search for something deeper. Like Rahu, he is a point where the Moon's path crosses the Sun's, always on the opposite side of the sky from Rahu.

Strong Ketu sees through things: calm under loss, sharp instinct, old talent that needs no teacher. Weak Ketu drifts: interest fades and people feel far away. Too much Ketu is the friend who is gone before anything starts.

Right now the monk sits in Leo with Jupiter, the teacher. They meet exactly on 11 November. On 5 December Ketu moves into Cancer.
