As above, so below: the sky over us now has happened six times since 1600

The line comes from the Emerald Tablet, an Arabic text from about 800 CE credited to Hermes and read in Latin by 1151: "That which is above is like to that which is below." India said it too. Charaka writes "puruṣo'yaṃ lokasammitaḥ", a person is a measure of the world (Charaka Samhita, Sharira 5.3). In Jyotish, Parashara reads the twelve signs as the body of Time, and Varahamihira read the same sky for whole countries: rain, harvests, famine and war.

So we tested it. Right now Jupiter, the teacher, meets Ketu, the monk, in Leo, the king's palace (graha is Sanskrit for "the one who grabs"). Our engine checked every day since 1600. It happened only six times before: 1635, 1672, 1766, 1802, 1896 and 1932.

October 1635, almost this exact sky: Massachusetts banished a preacher, Roger Williams, for his beliefs. He founded Rhode Island on freedom of conscience
June 1672, Saturn in Pisces like now: France invaded the Dutch Republic. The Dutch still call 1672 the disaster year
August 1896, the same degree as this November: a man took Zanzibar's palace without approval, and the war that followed lasted about 38 minutes. Famine and plague hit India
Autumn 1932: Gandhi fasted in prison until the Poona Pact, famine spread in Ukraine, the Depression hit bottom and America changed leader

What repeats: conscience against power, the palace contested, sudden strikes, and hardship that gives way to new freedoms.

What to watch, November to January: people of conscience stepping out, thrones tested, fights that flare fast (especially 12 to 16 November, when Mars joins), and food and money worries in places. These are rhymes to watch for; people still choose what they do with them.

So within: stand by what you've lived, don't take a seat you weren't offered, pick short clean fights, keep a small reserve this winter

Sutra: "When the sky rhymes, history rhymes"
