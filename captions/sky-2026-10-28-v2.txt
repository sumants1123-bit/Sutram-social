Today's Sky, Wednesday 28 October

The Moon is in Rohini, one of the 27 star groups it passes through each month. Rohini sits in Taurus, the sign where the Moon is strongest (Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra).

Rohini is a steady star. The old timing texts choose it for things meant to last: planting, building, steady work (Muhurta Chintamani).

Wednesday is Mercury's day, and Mercury, the planet of deals and messages, appears to move backward until 13 November. Finish and fix things today, and read twice before you sign.

Good for: steady work, planting, cooking, family
Go slow on: new contracts and launches
