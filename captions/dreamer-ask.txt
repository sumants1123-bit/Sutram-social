Everyone in the dream is the dreamer

In the film Inception, every person inside a dream is a projection: the dreamer's own mind, wearing faces. The city, the crowd and even the enemies all come from inside.

The Upanishads said this long ago. In a dream "there are no chariots there, no horses, no roads. He makes them all, for he is the maker" (Brihadaranyaka Upanishad 4.3.10).

Your birth chart works the same way. Each graha (Sanskrit for "the one who grabs") plays two parts at once: a part of you, and a person in your life.
The Sun, the king: your soul and your father
The Moon, the mother: your mind and your mother
Mars, the soldier: your courage and your brothers
Venus, the lover: your desire and your partner

Varahamihira sets out the grahas as one person, Kalapurusha, the Person of Time: soul, mind, strength, speech, knowledge, desire and sorrow (Brihat Jataka 2.1). Each house is a bhava, a "state of being": the 4th is your mother and your peace, the 7th your partner and your other half, the 10th your boss and your work.

In real life, the friend who always pushes you is your own Mars, out loud. The boss who checks every line is your own Saturn, the auditor. In the film, the projections fight back when the dream starts to change. In life, the hard people often show up just when a graha in you is changing.

Try this: think of the hardest person in your life right now and find their graha (father: Sun, mother: Moon, partner: Venus, boss: your 10th house). Treat that person a little better this week. The old remedies work the same way: honour your father for the Sun, care for your mother for the Moon.
