Read this weekend's sky with five Nadi rules

You learned five rules this week. Here they are on one sky, step by step, so you can do it yourself. Grahas (Sanskrit for "the ones who grab") are the characters.

Rule 1, same room. The soldier (Mars), the teacher (Jupiter) and the monk (Ketu) share Leo, within one degree of each other.

Rule 3, who walks in front. The teacher has the most degrees, so he leads. The soldier came in last and follows. On Saturday at 7:30 PM Toronto time he passes the monk, and late Sunday night he passes the teacher. From Monday the soldier leads, and advice turns into action.

Rule 2, ahead and behind. No graha sits behind Leo, in Cancer, so nothing holds this room back. Ahead, in Virgo, is the lover (Venus), who turns forward tonight.

Rule 4, face to face. Straight across, in Aquarius, sits the magician (Rahu).

Rule 5, looking back. The auditor (Saturn) walks backward in Pisces, so he also works from Aquarius. He joins the magician across the table.

Join them and you get one sentence. A hot, crowded room faces hype and old duties, while love starts moving again.

In real life this weekend, tempers run short around bosses and big egos. Bold claims get checked against old records. An old message or an old flame comes back. Slow answers win.

Now make it yours. Find Leo in your chart and say the sentence for that house. The last slides list all twelve rising signs. Write down what you expect, and check it on Monday.
