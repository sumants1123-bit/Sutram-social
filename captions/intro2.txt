Know yourself

That's what this page is about. Philosophy and astrology each play their own part.

Philosophy asks who you are. It teaches you to look within and see your own mind clearly. Ramana Maharshi taught people to keep asking that one question: Who am I?

Astrology shows how you're made. Your birth chart maps your nature, your strengths, your struggles and the timing of your life. Varahamihira calls the Sun the soul and the Moon the mind (Brihat Jataka 2.1).

Sutram means thread. It ties the two together for one purpose: to know yourself.

Here, every day:
Today's Sky: each morning, what the day is good for
A daily lesson: astrology, one clear step at a time
The Thread: Wednesdays, philosophy read through your chart

Start with your own chart. It's free at sutram.ca (link in bio).
