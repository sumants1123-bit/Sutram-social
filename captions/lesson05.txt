Lesson 5: Mercury

In Jyotish, Mercury is your words and your friends. Varahamihira calls it the speech of Time (Brihat Jataka 2.1), and Mantreswara puts learning, clever speech, kin and friends under it (Phaladeepika, chapter 2). It shows how you talk, text and learn.

Picture two friends. One is in every group chat, explains things so people get them, and talks her way to a good deal. That's strong Mercury. The other keeps a small, close circle, means well but it comes out wrong, and rewrites a text five times. That's weak Mercury. Too much Mercury is the friend who overthinks everything.

In Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, Mercury is strongest in Virgo (exalted), at home in Gemini and Virgo (its own signs) and weakest in Pisces (debilitated). Right now it's moving backward, until 13 November, a classic time for old friends to get back in touch.

The Rig Veda says the wise sifted speech with the mind, like flour through a sieve, and there friends knew friendship (10.71.2). Good words and good friends go together. In your chart, Mercury rules both.
