What is reality made of?

Samkhya, one of the oldest schools of Indian philosophy, answers with a map in layers. Its classic text is the Samkhya Karika of Ishvarakrishna, 72 verses from about 350 CE.

It sees two. Purusha, the seer: pure awareness that only watches. Prakriti, nature: everything that moves and can be seen (verses 3, 11, 19).

Near the seer, nature unfolds in layers, subtlest first (verse 22):
1. Mahat, the great one, also called buddhi: the intellect, which knows and decides
2. Ahamkara, the I, which says me and mine
3. Manas, the mind, which thinks, wishes and gathers what the senses bring
4. The senses: five to know, five to act
5. The body and the world, made of five elements: space, air, fire, water, earth

The mix-up: the light is the seer's, yet the layers seem awake and the seer seems to act (verse 20). So we say "I am angry" when anger is only moving in the layers.

The way out: see each layer as a layer. Like a dancer who stops once she has been seen, nature rests, and the seer stands free. Samkhya calls it kaivalya (verses 59, 66, 68).

In your chart: Parashara calls the five elements tattvas, the same word, and gives them to five grahas: Mars fire, Mercury earth, Jupiter space, Venus water, Saturn air (Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra 3.20).

In real life:
Tired body? The outer layer
Racing thoughts? The mind
"Why me?" The I
A clear call? The intellect
The one noticing all this? The seer
