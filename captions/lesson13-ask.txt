Lesson 13: Bhrigu Nandi Nadi, rule 3: who walks in front

When two grahas (Sanskrit for "the ones who grab") share a sign, Nadi asks who is leading. Every sign has 30 degrees.

Rule 3 says the graha with more degrees walks in front, and the one behind takes on the leader's nature.

Today it happens in the sky. The teacher (Jupiter) and the monk (Ketu) share Leo, and at 3:30 PM Toronto time the teacher passes the monk. Since 31 October the monk had more degrees, so the teacher walked behind him and became a bit of a monk: wisdom turning inward, less advice, more letting go. From today the teacher leads and the monk becomes a bit of a teacher: old instinct turns into advice, and you can teach what you let go of. This lasts until the monk leaves Leo on 5 December.

In your chart, find two grahas in one sign and compare their degrees. The one behind is learning from the one in front.
