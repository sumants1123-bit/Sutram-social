Lesson 6: Jupiter, the teacher

In Jyotish each graha (Sanskrit for "the one who grabs") is a character in your life's story. Jupiter is the teacher: wisdom, faith, mentors, savings and children. Varahamihira calls Jupiter the knowledge and happiness of Time (Brihat Jataka 2.1), and Mantreswara puts teachers, children, wisdom, good conduct and wealth under it (Phaladeepika, chapter 2). His Sanskrit names say the rest: Guru, "the heavy one", and Jiva, "life". Where he sits in your chart, things grow.

Picture two friends. One is the person everyone calls for advice. Her savings grow quietly, and a mentor always seems to turn up at the right time. That's strong Jupiter. The other knows a lot but nobody asks, money slips away, and faith comes and goes. That's weak Jupiter. Too much Jupiter is the friend who lectures.

In Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, Jupiter is strongest in Cancer (exalted), at home in Sagittarius and Pisces (its own signs) and weakest in Capricorn (debilitated). On 31 October the teacher walked into Leo, the king's palace. He stays until 24 January, goes back to Cancer to finish old lessons, and returns in June 2027 for the long stay. Teachers and real experts get the stage.

The Mundaka Upanishad says: to know the truth, go to a teacher with fuel in your hands (1.2.12). Students once carried firewood for the teacher's fire, so they arrived ready to work. In your chart, Jupiter shows where your teachers come from.
