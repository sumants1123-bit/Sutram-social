Lesson 14: Bhrigu Nandi Nadi, rule 4: face to face

Every sign has one sign straight across the circle from it, the 7th. Aries faces Libra, Leo faces Aquarius, Virgo faces Pisces.

Rule 4: grahas (Sanskrit for "the ones who grab") in opposite signs look straight at each other. It's a direct exchange, like two people across a table: a deal, a debate, a marriage.

Two pairs face each other in the sky now. The lover (Venus) in Virgo faces the auditor (Saturn) in Pisces: love and money under review. And from this morning the soldier (Mars) in Leo faces the magician (Rahu) in Aquarius: big moves and big hype, so check the facts.

Now read Leo with all four rules. Same room (rule 1) has the soldier joining the teacher (Jupiter) and the monk (Ketu). In front (rule 3) is the teacher, and the soldier came in last, so for now he follows. Face to face (rule 4), all three look at the magician. Ahead (rule 2), the lover waits in Virgo. Read together, a soldier walks into a quiet room, and across the table sits the magician.

In real life this weekend, a calm talk can turn sharp fast, most of all with a boss or a big ego. Bold promises get tested in public. From Friday to Monday all three are within one degree of each other, so keep your temper slow and act only on what you checked yourself.

To make it yours, find Leo in your chart. That house is the room. The house across from it is who you are dealing with.
