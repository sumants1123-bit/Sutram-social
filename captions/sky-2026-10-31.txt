Today's Sky, Saturday 31 October

The Moon is in Punarvasu, in Gemini. Punarvasu means "good again", the light coming back after a storm, and its deity is Aditi, mother of the gods (the Vedic star list in the Taittiriya Brahmana). It's a movable star, kept for travel and anything that needs to get moving (Muhurta Chintamani).

In real life: message the friend you lost touch with, reopen the plan you shelved, get back to the gym. At 2:40 PM the Moon moves into Cancer, its own sign, so the evening turns homey.

The big shift: Jupiter, the planet of growth and good teachers, moves into Leo today. Leo is confidence, children and creative work, so this season rewards people who show their work. Jupiter slips back into Cancer from 25 January to 26 June, then returns.

Mercury, Venus and Saturn are still moving backward, so read before you sign.

Good for: travel, picking up old plans
Go slow on: signing and big buys
Rahu Kaal, a poor time to start anything: 10:27 to 11:43 AM Toronto

Clocks go back an hour tonight. Enjoy the extra sleep.
