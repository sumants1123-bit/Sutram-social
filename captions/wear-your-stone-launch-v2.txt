Wear Your Stone: your personal gemstone report

Jyotish gives each graha (Sanskrit for "the one who grabs") its own stone (Phaladeepika, chapter 2):
Ruby: the king, Sun
Pearl: the mother, Moon
Red coral: the soldier, Mars
Emerald: the messenger, Mercury
Yellow sapphire: the teacher, Jupiter
Diamond: the lover, Venus
Blue sapphire: the auditor, Saturn
Hessonite: the magician, Rahu
Cat's eye: the monk, Ketu

In your chart, three of them work for you: the lords of your 1st, 5th and 9th houses (Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, chapter 34). Your rising sign decides who they are.

In real life it goes beyond the ring: studs for your ears, a pendant over your heart, beads of the lighter stones on your wrist, and plain silver for anklets and toe rings.

Your personal report shows your main stone and its two friends, the finger, metal and start dates for each, a stone for each part of life and for each goal, and the stones to skip. The pages shown come from a sample report.

Stones and their grahas are classical. Fitting them to your chart is Sutram's reading, with no promise about results.
