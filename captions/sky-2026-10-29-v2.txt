Today's Sky, Thursday 29 October

The Moon is in Mrigashira, the deer's head. A deer is always searching, so this star brings curiosity and a love of learning. At 12:40 PM the Moon moves from Taurus into Gemini, from steady ground into busy thoughts.

The old timing texts call Mrigashira a soft star, good for gentle things like music, art, friendship and learning (Muhurta Chintamani). Today is also the 4th day of the waning Moon, one of three "empty" lunar days in each half of the month, kept for finishing rather than starting.

Thursday is Jupiter's day, the planet of knowledge and happiness (Brihat Jataka 2.1). Mercury still appears to move backward until 13 November.

Good for: study, music, finishing things
Go slow on: new starts and signing
