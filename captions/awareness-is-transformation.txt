Awareness is transformation

Try this now. Notice how you are sitting. Don't change anything, just notice.

Something already moved. The moment you noticed, your back straightened by itself.

So which came first? Did awareness straighten you, or did you notice and then fix it? Look for the one who fixed it. Is there an I in there, or only noticing?

In Jyotish the Sun is the soul (atma, the self) and the Moon is the mind (Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, chapter 3). The Moon has no light of its own. It shines only because the Sun lights it. The mind works the same way: awareness is the light it shines with.

The Upanishads say it in two words: prajnanam brahma, "awareness is Brahman", the whole of what is (Aitareya Upanishad 3.1.3).

Try it today with your breath, your words, your temper. Notice, and watch what changes. Awareness is everything.
