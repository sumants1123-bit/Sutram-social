Lesson 1: what, how, where

Every placement in your birth chart is a short sentence with three parts: the planet, the sign and the house. Learn to read that sentence and you can read any chart.

The planet is what: the part of you that's acting. Varahamihira lays it out in the Brihat Jataka (2.1): the Sun is the soul of Time, the Moon its mind, Mars its strength, Mercury its speech, Jupiter its knowledge and happiness, Venus its desire, Saturn its sorrow. Rahu and Ketu are the Moon's two shadow points. Rahu is the hunger for more, and Ketu is the urge to let go.

The sign is how: the climate a planet lives in. Parashara gives every sign an element (Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, on the signs). Fire signs (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius) act quickly and boldly. Earth signs (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn) act slowly and practically. Air signs (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius) think and connect. Water signs (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces) feel and protect. That's why Mars in Aries charges ahead, while Mars in Cancer fights hardest for home.

The house is where: the area of life. From the 1st house to the 12th: self, money, effort, home, children, work and health, partner, change, luck, career, gains, and letting go (Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, on the houses).

Put them together. Mars in Leo in the 10th house: your drive (Mars) acts with pride and wants to lead (Leo), and it shows up in your career (10th house).

Try it with your Moon. Which sign is it in, and which house? Write your sentence in the comments.
