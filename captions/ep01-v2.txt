Body, mind, soul

This is The Thread, our weekly series. Each Wednesday: one idea from philosophy, and where it shows up in your birth chart.

Philosophy says you are more than a body. The Upanishads describe a person in layers, from the body inward to the soul (Taittiriya Upanishad, chapter 2).

Astrology reads the same layers. Parashara teaches reading every chart from three starting points (Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, the Sudarshana chakra):
Ascendant: your body, how you meet the world
Moon: your mind, how you feel
Sun: your soul, what you live for

So one person holds all three. Ascendant in Leo meets the world warmly and likes to lead. Moon in Cancer feels deeply and needs a safe home. Sun in Capricorn lives for duty and lasting work.
