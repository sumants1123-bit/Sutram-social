Lesson 12: Bhrigu Nandi Nadi, rule 2: ahead and behind

The signs run in order: Aries, then Taurus, then Gemini, and on around the circle. Nadi reads the signs on either side of each graha (Sanskrit for "the one who grabs").

Rule 2: the sign ahead is where a graha is heading: its next chapter, what it builds next. The sign behind is what it carries: its past, its training, what it spends on. In chart language, the 2nd sign from a graha and the 12th.

In the sky today, look around Leo, where the teacher (Jupiter) and the monk (Ketu) sit together. Behind them, in Cancer, is the soldier (Mars). Ahead of them, in Virgo, is the lover (Venus). So the teacher carries the soldier's fire and walks toward the lover's world: money, comfort, beauty. On Thursday morning the soldier walks into Leo himself.

Try it on your chart: find your Moon, then look one sign ahead and one sign behind. Who sits there?
