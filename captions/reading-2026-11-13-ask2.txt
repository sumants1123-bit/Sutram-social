Read this weekend's sky with five Nadi rules

Four parts: the sky, rule by rule, the reading, your chart

PART 1 · THE SKY
Friday 13 to Sunday 15 November, Toronto time. Leo holds three grahas (Sanskrit for "the ones who grab") less than one degree apart: the soldier (Mars), the monk (Ketu) and the teacher (Jupiter).

PART 2 · RULE BY RULE
Rule 1, same room. The three in Leo act as one. Push, let go and advise, all at once.
Rule 2, ahead and behind. Cancer, behind Leo, is empty, so nothing holds this room back. Ahead, in Virgo, the lover (Venus) turns forward tonight around 7:30 PM.
Rule 3, who walks in front. The teacher has the most degrees, so he leads. The soldier came in last and walks fastest.
Rule 4, face to face. The magician (Rahu) sits in Aquarius, straight across from Leo.
Rule 5, looking back. The auditor (Saturn) walks backward in Pisces, so he also works from Aquarius and joins the magician across the table.

PART 3 · THE READING
Join them and you get one sentence. A hot, crowded room faces hype and old duties, while love starts moving again.
In real life this weekend, tempers run short around bosses and big egos. Bold claims get checked against old records. An old message or an old flame comes back. Slow answers win.
Watch it move. On Saturday the soldier passes the monk. Sunday night into Monday he passes the teacher. From Monday the soldier leads, and advice turns into action.

PART 4 · YOUR CHART
Find Leo in your chart and say the sentence for that house. Slides 12 and 13 list all twelve rising signs. Write down what you expect and check it on Monday.
