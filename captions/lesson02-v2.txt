Lesson 2: the Sun

In Jyotish the Sun is your self. Varahamihira calls it the soul (Brihat Jataka 2.1). It shows your confidence and purpose, your father and the people in charge of you, and your health and energy.

In Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, the Sun is strongest in Aries (exalted), at home in Leo (its own sign) and weakest in Libra (debilitated).

By house, it does best in the 10th, the house of career. Parashara gives the Sun full strength there, so a 10th house Sun tends to live a visible life.

The Upanishads say of the Self: "That is the pure light of all lights" (Mundaka Upanishad 2.2.9). In your chart, the Sun stands for that light.
