Week ahead: Jupiter in Leo, for your Moon sign

Jupiter moved into Leo on Saturday. In Jyotish, Jupiter is Guru, the teacher, and where it walks in your chart, things grow. To see where, count from your Moon sign, the sign the Moon was in when you were born. The classics give Jupiter its best results in the 2nd, 5th, 7th, 9th and 11th from the Moon (Phaladeepika, chapter 26).

Aries Moon, 5th: kids, study, romance
Taurus Moon, 4th: home, mother, repairs
Gemini Moon, 3rd: effort, short trips
Cancer Moon, 2nd: savings, family, food
Leo Moon, 1st: you in focus; pace yourself
Virgo Moon, 12th: spending, travel, rest
Libra Moon, 11th: gains and good friends
Scorpio Moon, 10th: heavier work; stay humble
Sagittarius Moon, 9th: luck, teachers, travel
Capricorn Moon, 8th: quiet change; health care
Aquarius Moon, 7th: partner, clients, deals
Pisces Moon, 6th: routine, debts, fitness

This first visit is short. Jupiter slips back into Cancer on 24 January and returns on 25 June 2027.

Also this week: the Moon joins Jupiter in Leo from Monday night to Wednesday, a good time to ask for advice. Venus slips back into Virgo on Thursday, so go easy on spending. Diwali is next Sunday, on the New Moon night.

The Katha Upanishad says: "Arise, awake; find the great ones and learn" (1.3.14). Philosophy says find a teacher. Jupiter shows where one finds you.
