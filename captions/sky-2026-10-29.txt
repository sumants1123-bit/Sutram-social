Today's sky, Thursday 29 October

The Moon is in Mrigashira, the deer's head. Its deity is Soma, the Moon itself, and its ruler is Mars. A deer is always searching, so this star brings curiosity, restlessness and a love of learning. At 12:40 PM the Moon crosses from Taurus into Gemini, from steady ground into busy thoughts.

The muhurta texts count Mrigashira among the soft stars, chosen for gentle and friendly things: music, art, friendship, learning (Muhurta Chintamani). Today is also the fourth day of the waning Moon, one of the three "empty" lunar days (the 4th, 9th and 14th), which the same texts keep for clearing and finishing rather than starting.

Thursday belongs to Jupiter, the teacher; Varahamihira gives Jupiter knowledge and happiness (Brihat Jataka 2.1). Mercury is still retrograde in Libra until 13 November.

Good for: study, music, kind conversations, clearing and finishing
Go slow on: starting new ventures and signing
