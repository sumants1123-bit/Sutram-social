Know yourself, five ways

Five traditions, far apart in time and place, give the same instruction.

Greece: "Know thyself", carved at the entrance to Apollo's temple at Delphi
Socrates: "The unexamined life is not worth living" (Plato, Apology 38a)
The Upanishads: "Know that one Self, and leave other words" (Mundaka Upanishad 2.2.5)
The Tao: "He who knows other men is discerning; he who knows himself is intelligent" (Tao Te Ching 33, James Legge)
The Sufis: "Whoever knows himself knows his Lord", a saying they treasure

Philosophy tells you to look within. Your birth chart gives you something to look at: your nature, your mind and your timing.
