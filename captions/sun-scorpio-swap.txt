Current sky: the Sun moves into Scorpio and exchanges signs with Mars

On Monday 16 November at 9:13 AM Toronto time, the Sun walks from Libra into Scorpio and stays there until 15 December. That crossing is a Sankranti, Sanskrit for "crossing over".

Scorpio is Mars's sign. Mars sits in Leo, the Sun's sign. Each one sits in the other's sign, which is an exchange of signs (parivartana in Sanskrit).

How Bhrigu Nadi reads an exchange
1. Read each graha in its own sign: the Sun in Leo, Mars in Scorpio. Both start out strong
2. The two are now bound together. The Sun is the king (authority, government, father). Mars is the commander (energy, courage, brothers)
3. Check who sits with each one. In Leo the Sun meets Jupiter, the guide. On 2 December Mercury (intellect) joins Mars in Scorpio

In the sky itself, the Sun sits in Mars's sign, so authority turns into action. Mars sits in the Sun's sign, so effort goes to those in charge.

What to do from 16 November to 15 December: lead, and take advice. Put your energy into one hard job, and keep your ego in check
