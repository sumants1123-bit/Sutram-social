Swapped houses: the Sun and Mars trade places

On Monday 16 November at 9:13 AM Toronto time, the Sun (the king) walks from Libra into Scorpio. That crossing is a Sankranti, Sanskrit for "crossing over". There are twelve a year, and Makar Sankranti in January is the famous one. He leaves Libra, his weakest sign, and stays in Scorpio until 15 December.

Scorpio is the house of Mars (the soldier). Mars sits in Leo, the Sun's house. Each one is a guest in the other's house. The classics call this parivartana, an exchange of signs.

How Nadi reads it: send each one home in your mind. Read the Sun in Leo and Mars in Scorpio. At home, both count as strong. Then see who each one meets there. In Leo the Sun meets Jupiter (the teacher), who adds advice. On 2 December Mercury (the messenger) joins Mars in Scorpio and adds talk.

Until 15 December: make the call you have been putting off, finish one hard job properly, and ask a mentor before the big move. Write it down and check it on 15 December.
