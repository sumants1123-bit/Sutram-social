Lesson 4: Mars

In Jyotish, Mars is your get-up-and-go. Varahamihira calls it the strength of Time (Brihat Jataka 2.1). It shows your energy and courage, how you face a fight or a deadline, and your brothers and sisters.

Picture two friends. One is first to say "let's go", sends back the wrong order and finishes what she starts. That's strong Mars. The other plans, and plans, and plans, eats the wrong order, and quits the gym in week two. That's weak Mars. Too much Mars is the friend with the short temper.

In Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, Mars is strongest in Capricorn (exalted), at home in Aries and Scorpio (its own signs) and weakest in Cancer (debilitated). Mars is in Cancer right now, until 12 November. If you've felt tired or short-fused lately, that's part of the weather.

The Gita says: "Lift yourself by yourself; do not let yourself sink" (6.5). Philosophy tells you to rise. Your Mars shows how you rise. Same goal: know yourself.
