Happy Diwali

Tonight is Amavasya, the new Moon. The Sun and Moon meet in Libra and there is no moonlight at all, so we light our own. That is the whole idea of Diwali: the darkest night of the month gets the most lamps.

For Lakshmi, keep it simple. Clean one corner, light one lamp and give one thing away.

In the sky, Mars, the soldier, is at his weakest point tonight, deep in Cancer. Choose calm over noise. He walks into Leo on 12 November.

Shubh Deepavali. May your year be bright.
