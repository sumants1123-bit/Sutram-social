Four houses in your chart get better with age

The 3rd, 6th, 10th and 11th houses are called upachaya, which means "increase" (Varahamihira, Brihat Jataka, chapter 1). Whatever sits in these houses tends to start slow and grow with effort.

What each one covers in modern life:
3rd: your courage and your own hustle (the side project, the cold email, the first video you post)
6th: your daily grind (the job you show up to, the gym routine, how you handle a difficult coworker)
10th: your career (your title, your reputation at work, what people know you for)
11th: your income and network (raises, clients, the friend who refers you for a job)

This is why the old texts are kind to "hard" planets here. Saturn and Mars usually bring pressure. In the 3rd, 6th or 11th, that pressure turns into stamina. Think of a tough coach: unpleasant when you're 22, the reason you're good at 35.

Sutra: "Some parts of your chart are meant to start rough"

Practical remedy: find out which of these four houses has a planet in your chart. If life in that area felt slow in your twenties, that was the warm-up. Pick one habit that feeds it (one networking message a week for the 11th, one fixed workout routine for the 6th) and give it three honest years before you judge it.
