Mercury retrograde, house by house

Mercury turned retrograde in Libra on 24 October and turns direct on 13 November, still in Libra.

In the classics, a retrograde planet gains strength. Parashara gives a planet moving backward its full strength of motion (Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, on planetary strength), so it works harder than usual, often on things left unfinished. Mercury speaks for words, trade, learning and nerves; Varahamihira calls it the speech of Time (Brihat Jataka 2.1).
