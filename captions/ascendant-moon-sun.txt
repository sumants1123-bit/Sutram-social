Your Ascendant, and why you read it with the Moon and Sun

Your Ascendant is the sign that was rising in the east when you were born. It shows your body and the path your life takes. Parashara calls the 1st house tanu, the body (Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra). It changes every two hours or so.

The Moon shows how you feel and react, day to day. Varahamihira calls the Moon the mind and the Sun the soul (Brihat Jataka 2.1). That's why daily transits are counted from your Moon sign (Mantreswara, Phaladeepika, chapter 26).

So two people with the same Ascendant can look alike and feel very different. A Leo Ascendant with the Moon in Aries reacts fast and says it straight. A Leo Ascendant with the Moon in Pisces takes in everyone's feelings.

Parashara reads every chart from all three: the Ascendant, the Moon and the Sun. When you read the Sun and Moon along with the Ascendant, the chart starts to make much more sense.
