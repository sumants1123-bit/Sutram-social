Your 12th house is your rest budget

Most people fear the 12th house: loss, bills, hospitals. Here is a more useful way to read it. The 12th house shows what you have to spend, give up or switch off from, so you can keep going.

For one person it's sleep: every short night costs them the next day. For another it's distance: a life in a new city or country, and the price is missing family dinners and festivals back home. For another it's quiet time: a weekend offline, a prayer hour, giving to a cause.

Parashara calls the 12th vyaya, the house of spending (Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra), and the old texts give it sleep as well. That pairing makes sense in city life. When you don't rest, you pay for it anyway: the late-night takeout, the cancelled plans, the clinic visit, the bad decision made tired.

Sutra: "What you won't spend on rest, you will spend on recovery"

A strong 12th house often makes you good at letting go: switching off, travelling, living abroad, healing work, helping people quietly.

Practical remedy: open your bank app and look at the last three months. Circle every charge that came from being tired, and add it up. Then put two hours of real rest in your calendar this week, before anything else gets booked.
