Lesson 11: Bhrigu Nandi Nadi, rule 1: same room

From today the lessons teach Bhrigu Nandi Nadi from scratch, one rule a day. It's an old way of reading a chart, named for the sage Bhrigu and for Nandi, Shiva's gatekeeper in the old stories. It looks at the sign each graha (Sanskrit for "the one who grabs") sits in, and who sits with whom.

Rule 1: same sign, same room. Two grahas in one sign live together, and their stories mix. In Nadi this is the strongest link.

What it can look like:
The teacher (Jupiter) with the soldier (Mars): a coach who pushes you, a lawyer who fights
The lover (Venus) with the auditor (Saturn): love that starts slow and lasts
The messenger (Mercury) with the lover (Venus): words that charm, in sales, in design, in the first text

In the sky today, three share Libra: the king (Sun), the mother (Moon) and the messenger (Mercury). The mother leaves at 4:20 PM, Toronto time. Over in Leo, the teacher (Jupiter) and the monk (Ketu) share a room, and they meet exactly on Wednesday.

Which signs in your chart hold two or more grahas? Those are the rooms where your stories mix.
