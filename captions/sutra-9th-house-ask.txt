The 9th house: luck that teaches

In Jyotish the 9th house is called bhagya, "fortune". It holds your father, your teachers and mentors, your faith, long trips and higher study.

They share a house because luck usually reaches you through people who taught you something. The teacher (Jupiter) is the natural karaka of this house: the graha (Sanskrit for "the one who grabs") that stands for it in every chart.

What it covers in modern life:
Fortune: the lucky break, the job that found you
Teachers: the professor who vouched for you, the boss who trained you
Faith: what you believe, your reason to keep going when it gets hard
Long trips: the year abroad, the degree that changed how you think

Sutra: "Luck is a teacher you listened to"

Practical remedy: find your 9th house and the grahas sitting in it. This week, write to one teacher who changed your life and tell them what you did with it. Thursday is the teacher's day: Guruvar, Jupiter's day.
