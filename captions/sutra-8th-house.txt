The 8th house: other people's money

The 8th house scares people: death, accidents, scandal. Parashara reads longevity from it (Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra), which is where the scary reputation comes from. In everyday life it shows the parts of your life that depend on other people's money and other people's decisions.

For one person it's a spouse's income: when their job changes, your plans change too. For another it's shared family property: the house back home that five relatives have a say in. For another it's the paperwork nobody enjoys: insurance, taxes, a loan you co-signed, a business partner's debt.

Here is why the 8th covers your partner's money. The 7th house is your partner. The 2nd house is money. Count your partner's house as their 1st, and their 2nd house lands on your 8th. Astrologers call this bhavat bhavam, house from house, and use it all the time.

Sutra: "You can't control the 8th house. You can prepare for it"

A strong 8th house makes people calm when things go wrong. Surgeons, researchers, investigators, insurance and tax people, the cousin everyone calls in a crisis.

Practical remedy: make one folder your family could open if you were unreachable for a month: bank accounts, insurance policies, important documents, and who to call for what. Tell one person where it is. Update it every Diwali, along with the cleaning.
