Mercury retrograde, house by house

From 24 October to 13 November, Mercury appears to move backward through Libra. Mercury rules words, deals and thinking; Varahamihira calls it the speech of Time (Brihat Jataka 2.1).

In the classics, a planet moving backward gains strength (Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, on planetary strength). So Mercury works harder, and often on old matters: old drafts, old contacts, old plans.

Where it lands depends on your Ascendant. Swipe to slides 3 and 4, find your Ascendant sign, and see which part of your life Mercury is going back over.

How to use it:
Finish before you start
Reread before you send, and back things up
If something old returns, look at it properly

Mercury moves forward again on Friday 13 November.
