Sade Sati: Saturn's audit

Sade Sati is running right now for three Moon signs: Aquarius, Pisces and Aries. Saturn spends about two and a half years in each sign. Sade Sati is the stretch when it passes through three signs in a row: the one before your Moon sign, your Moon sign, and the one after. Three signs, about seven and a half years. The old texts on transits list Saturn's walk through these three signs from your Moon among its difficult ones (Phaladeepika, chapter 26).

People call it bad luck. It works more like an audit at work. Saturn checks what you built and how you built it. Things built on shortcuts get tested. Things built on steady effort usually hold.

For one person it's a job they stayed in out of fear, which finally stops working. For another it's health habits running on credit: years of late nights and skipped meals that start sending the bill. For another it's a relationship on autopilot, which now needs real attention.

Sutra: "Saturn takes away what you were only holding out of habit"

Where you are now:
Aquarius Moon: the last stretch, ending early 2028
Pisces Moon: the middle and heaviest part, until June 2027
Aries Moon: just started, running to 2032

What Saturn rewards: routine, patience, honest work, and care for elders and for the people who work for you

Practical remedy: write down three things you keep doing only out of habit, and drop one this month. Then give every Saturday one hour of real work on the thing you keep putting off. Saturday is Saturn's day, and Saturn respects people who show up.
