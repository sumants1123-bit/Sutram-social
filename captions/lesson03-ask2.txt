Lesson 3: the Moon, the mother

In Jyotish each graha (Sanskrit for "the one who grabs") is a character in your life's story. The Moon is the mother: your mind. Varahamihira calls it the mind of Time (Brihat Jataka 2.1). It shows how you feel and react, your mother and your home, and how you rest.

In Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, the Moon is strongest in Taurus (exalted), at home in Cancer (its own sign) and weakest in Scorpio (debilitated).

Its phase matters too. A bright, growing Moon is gentle and strong; a dark, fading Moon is weaker. So check whether you were born under a growing or a fading Moon.

The Mahabharata explains the phases with a story. The 27 stars were Daksha's daughters, all married to the Moon. The Moon loved Rohini most, so Daksha cursed him to waste away. The curse was softened, and ever since the Moon fades and grows again (Shalya Parva).

Try it with your Moon. Which sign is it in, and was it bright or dark? Tell me in the comments.
