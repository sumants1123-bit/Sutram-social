Lesson 1: what, how, where

Every placement in your chart has three parts. Learn these and you can read any chart.

The planet is what: the part of you that acts. The Sun is your self, the Moon your mind, Mars your drive (Brihat Jataka 2.1).

The sign is how: the way it acts. Fire signs act boldly, earth signs steadily, air signs think and connect, water signs feel and protect (Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, on the signs).

The house is where: the area of life. The 1st house is you, the 4th your home, the 7th your partner, the 10th your career.

Put it together. Mars in Leo in the 10th house: your drive leads, with pride, in your career.
