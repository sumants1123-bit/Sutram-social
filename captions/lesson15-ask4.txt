Lesson 15 · Bhrigu Nandi Nadi, rule 5: looking back

Three parts: the rule, today's sky, your chart

PART 1 · THE RULE
Some weeks a graha (Sanskrit for "the one who grabs") seems to walk backward through the sky. Jyotish calls it vakri, retrograde. Rule 5: a graha walking backward also works from the sign behind it, like someone turning back to finish old business.
Read it with three questions. Who is walking backward? Which sign is behind it? Who lives in that sign?

PART 2 · TODAY'S SKY
The messenger (Mercury) turned forward this morning. The lover (Venus) turns forward tonight around 7:30 PM Toronto time. The auditor (Saturn) keeps walking backward until 10 December.
The lover turned back on 3 October in Libra and backed only 1°23′ into Virgo. Until 5 November she looked back to Virgo. Since then she has looked back to Leo, where the teacher (Jupiter), the monk (Ketu) and the soldier (Mars) live.
The reading: old loves and old deals came back. The ex who texted, the refund you chased. This week they came back as a lesson. From tonight she walks forward, home in Libra on 22 November. Write down what you expect and check it that day.

PART 3 · YOUR CHART
Until 10 December the auditor works from Pisces and Aquarius. Slides 8 and 9 show which two rooms of your chart that is, by rising sign. Old duties there get a second look.
