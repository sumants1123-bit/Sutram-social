When the teacher meets the monk

In Jyotish the word is graha, Sanskrit for "the one who grabs": nine forces, each holding one part of your life. Bhrigu Nandi Nadi reads them as characters in a story.

Jupiter is the teacher: what you learned, your faith, your mentors, your savings. Ketu is the monk: what you outgrew, what you know without being taught, and the art of letting go.

Rule 1, same room. When two grahas sit in the same sign, they share a room and their stories mix. In Nadi this is the strongest link there is.

Rule 2, who walks in front. Inside the room, count the degrees. The graha with more degrees walks in front, and the one behind takes on the leader's nature.

Monk in front: the teacher becomes a monk. You stop following advice you were given and trust what you've lived. A mentor steps back, savings get given away, faith goes quiet and personal.

Teacher in front: the monk becomes a teacher. What you went through quietly becomes what you can teach, like the burnout survivor who starts coaching or the quiet meditator who opens a class.

Right now Jupiter and Ketu share Leo. From 31 October to 11 November the monk has more degrees, so the teacher turns monk. From 11 November to 5 December the teacher walks in front, so the monk turns teacher.

Sutra: "Let go first, then teach what's left"
