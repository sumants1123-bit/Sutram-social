Today's Sky, Friday 30 October

The Moon is in Ardra, in Gemini. Ardra means "moist", and its deity is Rudra, the storm (the Vedic star list in the Taittiriya Brahmana). It's one of the sharp stars: the old timing texts keep it for endings, clearing out and hard, honest work, and avoid it for celebrations and new starts (Muhurta Chintamani).

Friday is Venus's day, and Venus is still moving backward until 13 November, so go easy on big purchases and new commitments. It's the 5th day of the waning Moon.

Good for: endings and research
Go slow on: celebrations and new starts
