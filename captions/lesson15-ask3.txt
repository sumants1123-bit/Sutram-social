Lesson 15 · Bhrigu Nandi Nadi, rule 5: looking back

Four parts: the rule, this week's sky, a real case, your chart

PART 1 · THE RULE
Some weeks a graha (Sanskrit for "the one who grabs") seems to walk backward through the sky. Jyotish calls it vakri, retrograde. Rule 5 says a graha walking backward works from two signs at once: the sign it stands in and the sign behind it. Think of someone who hears a shout, turns and goes back to help. Old business pulls it back before it moves on.

PART 2 · THIS WEEK'S SKY
Three steps. Who is walking backward? Which sign is behind it? Who lives in that sign?
The lover (Venus) turned backward on 3 October in Libra, backed into Virgo on 5 November, and turns forward tonight around 7:30 PM Toronto time, only 1°23′ inside Virgo. For five weeks she also worked from Virgo. This past week she also worked from Leo, the room of the teacher (Jupiter) and the monk (Ketu).
The reading: old loves and old money matters came back. The ex who texted, the refund you chased. This week they showed up as a lesson. From tonight she walks forward, and she is home in Libra on 22 November. Write down what you expect and check it that day.

PART 3 · A REAL CASE
23 August 2023, 6:04 PM India time: Chandrayaan-3 lands near the Moon's south pole, four years after India's first lander crashed. The auditor (Saturn) was walking backward in Aquarius, so he also worked from Capricorn, his other home sign: old work, tried again. Straight across in Leo stood the king (Sun) and the messenger (Mercury): a country's work, checked live in front of everyone. Four days later the king and the auditor stood exactly face to face. The sky set the scene. People did the work.

PART 4 · YOUR CHART
