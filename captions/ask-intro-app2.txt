Meet Ask Sutram: ask your birth chart anything

Ask Sutram is the part of Sutram you can talk to. Type or say your question, in English or Hinglish, and it reads your birth chart first, then answers in plain words.

Every morning there's your chart today: which parts of your life are active, and this week's window, with an "Ask why" button for the reason behind it.

The Studio puts every instrument in one room: My Timing, My Chart, My Windows, Muhurta for picking a good day, Together for couples, Shubh Muhurat for weddings and housewarmings, Build a Plan for a ninety-day goal, and My Year Ahead.

Or ask straight from search: "When is my next good career window?" The answer comes from your own chart. Sutram's engine computes every position, date and window, and the AI explains them.

Try it with one question that's been on your mind.
