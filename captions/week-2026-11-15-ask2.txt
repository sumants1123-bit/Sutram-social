Week ahead: the king gets stronger

Four parts: the move, the rule, your Moon sign, the week

PART 1 · THE MOVE
On Monday 16 November at 9:13 AM Toronto time the Sun moves from Libra into Scorpio. In Jyotish the Sun is the king, and Libra is the sign where he is weakest (Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, chapter 3), so this is the king walking out of the wrong room. He stays in Scorpio until 15 December.

PART 2 · THE RULE
Count from your Moon sign, the sign the Moon was in when you were born. Your own sign is 1. The classics give the Sun his best results in the 3rd, 6th, 10th and 11th from the Moon (Phaladeepika, chapter 26). Virgo Moon: Virgo 1, Libra 2, Scorpio 3, so this is one of his best spots.

PART 4 · THE WEEK
Monday: before dawn the soldier (Mars) passes the teacher (Jupiter) in Leo, and at 9:13 AM the king enters Scorpio. Friday afternoon: the mother (Moon) meets the auditor (Saturn) in Pisces, a good time to finish one chore. Sunday: the lover (Venus) walks home into Libra, her own sign.

The Katha Upanishad says: "When he shines, everything shines after him" (2.2.15). The line is about the Self, the light behind every light. In your chart, the Sun shows where you shine.
