A graha walking backward is a second draft

Picture someone walking home who looks back, sees a person drowning, changes path and goes to save them. That's a graha walking backward. A graha (Sanskrit for "the one who grabs") is one of the nine characters in your chart, and Sanskrit calls one that walks backward vakri, "the crooked one". It turns around and goes back for what was missed.

Nothing in the sky really reverses. From Earth, a graha seems to walk backward when we overtake it, or it overtakes us, and that happens when it's at its closest to us. Parashara gives a graha walking backward full strength of motion (Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, on strength). In real life that strength often shows up as going back and doing it better.

Right now three are walking backward: the messenger (Mercury) and the lover (Venus) until 13 November, and the auditor (Saturn) until 10 December. Some people are born with one or more of them that way.

For one person it's the messenger walking backward: they rewrite every email and end up the best editor in the office
For another it's the lover walking backward: love and money choices get a second look, and the second choice is the one that lasts
For another it's the auditor walking backward: old duties come back to be finished properly, often later in life

Sutra: "Some gifts arrive as second drafts"

Practical remedy: find out if any graha in your chart walks backward. In that part of life, stop expecting to get it right the first time. Build in a review: sleep on the email, wait a week on the purchase, look at the plan again in a month.
