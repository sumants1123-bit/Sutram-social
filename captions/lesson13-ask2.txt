Lesson 13: Bhrigu Nandi Nadi, rule 3: who walks in front

When two grahas (Sanskrit for "the ones who grab") share a sign, Nadi asks who is leading. Every sign has 30 degrees.

Rule 3 says the graha with more degrees walks in front, and the one behind takes on the leader's nature.

Today it happens in the sky. The teacher (Jupiter) and the monk (Ketu) both sit near the first degree of Leo. Since 31 October the monk had more degrees, so the teacher walked behind him and became a bit of a monk: wisdom turning inward, less advice, more letting go. At 3:30 PM Toronto time the teacher passes him. From then the teacher leads and the monk becomes a bit of a teacher.

Now join it with the first two rules. Same room (rule 1) gives the teacher and the monk in Leo. In front (rule 3) is the teacher, from today. Behind them (rule 2) is the soldier (Mars) in Cancer, for one more day. Ahead is the lover (Venus) in Virgo, checking the bill. Read together, what you let go of becomes what you can teach.

In real life, before today you went quiet and held back. From today you can say what you learned, and people start asking how you got through it. This lasts until the monk leaves Leo on 5 December.

To make it yours, find Leo in your chart. That house is where the new voice shows up. Then find two grahas in one sign and compare their degrees. The one behind is learning from the one in front.
