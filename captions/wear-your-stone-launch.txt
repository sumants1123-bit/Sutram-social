Wear Your Stone: a new Sutram reading

Where do gems come from? Varahamihira of Ujjain gave three answers in the Brihat Samhita (chapter 80, verse 3). Some said gems are the bones of the demon Bala. Some said they are the bones of the sage Dadhichi. Others said they are simply stones of the earth.

Jyotish gives each graha (Sanskrit for "the one who grabs") its own stone (Phaladeepika, chapter 2):
Ruby: the king, Sun
Pearl: the mother, Moon
Red coral: the soldier, Mars
Emerald: the messenger, Mercury
Yellow sapphire: the teacher, Jupiter
Diamond: the lover, Venus
Blue sapphire: the auditor, Saturn
Hessonite: the magician, Rahu
Cat's eye: the monk, Ketu

In your chart, three of them work for you: the lords of your 1st, 5th and 9th houses (Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, chapter 34). Your rising sign decides who they are. Sutram then checks each pick in your D9 and D10 charts, your life chapters and the sky ahead.

In real life it goes beyond the ring: studs for your ears, a pendant over your heart, beads of the lighter stones on your wrist, and plain silver for anklets and toe rings.
